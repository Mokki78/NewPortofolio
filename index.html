MY WORK

This is a collection of my best work so far from my time studying UI Design and front-end development at Noroff School of technology & Digital media.

PHOTOGRAPHER MENDEN

This is my exam project assignment from UI Design at Noroff School of Technology & Digital media. The assignment was to make a visual profile, or redesing the current, with logo, colors, fonts and icons. After that we were to make a brand style guide, and design a website and a brand style system to go with it.



We had about 7 weeks to finish the project, and I worked alone for this one.

ABOUT

I am a web designer that codes and takes pictures, based in Larvik, Norway. I love colors, typography, photography and good design .

I also think that UX, and a good user experience is crucial when designing. If it does not work for the people it is intended for, it is not good design.

I have spent the last years of my life dedicated to learning websites. I have learned how to design them, UX, best practices, how to build them in code and how to make them visible online, which also is a crucial part. After finishing my last year at Noroff, I am now looking forward to my next project.

Maybe that will be with you?

MY WORK

Solar Smart

This was my semester project assignment at Noroff School of Technology and Digital Media. The task was to create a complete visual profile for a company selling solar panels to both private customers and businesses. The visual identity included a logo, color palette, typography, icons, elevation, and other design elements.


After establishing the visual profile, I designed a website, a brand style guide, and a brand system, and documented all design decisions throughout the process.


The project duration was four weeks, and I worked independently. The final result was graded with an A.

ABOUT

I’m a web designer with skills in coding and a creative eye for photography, based in Larvik, Norway, with a passion for colors, typography, and creating beautiful, functional designs. For me, great design goes beyond aesthetics—it’s about crafting seamless user experiences. If a design doesn’t work for its intended audience, it isn’t effective. Over the past few years, I’ve dedicated myself to mastering the art of web design. From understanding user experience (UX) principles and best practices to coding and optimizing websites for visibility online, I’ve gained a well-rounded skill set to bring ideas to life.

Now I am looking forward to my next project. Maybe that will be with you?

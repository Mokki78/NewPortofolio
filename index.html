MY WORK

This is a collection of my best work so far from my time studying UI Design and front-end development at Noroff School of Technology & Digital media.

PHOTOGRAPHER MENDEN

This is my exam project assignment from UI Design at Noroff School of Technology & Digital media. The assignment was to make a visual profile, or redesign the current design. Make a logo, color palette, choose the typography and icons that fits the brand. After that we made a brand style guide and a brand style system to showcase our choices, and we designed a website for the brand using the visual profile we designed.


We had about 7 weeks to finish the project, and I worked alone, and got an A for my work.

SOLAR SMART

This was my assignment for the semester project at Noroff school of Technology n& Digital media. The assignment was to make the visual profile for a company selling solar panels to both private customers as well as to businesses. This included a logo, color palette, fonts, icons, elevation etc. After that we were tasked to make a website, a brand style guide and a brand style system, and document all of our choices.


We had 4 weeks to finish, and I worked alone. Got an A for my work.

ABOUT

I’m a web designer with skills in coding and a creative eye for photography, based in Larvik, Norway, with a passion for colors, typography, and creating beautiful, functional designs.


For me, great design goes beyond aesthetics—it’s about crafting seamless user experiences. If a design doesn’t work for its intended audience, it isn’t effective.


Over the past few years, I’ve dedicated myself to mastering the art of web design. From understanding user experience (UX) principles and best practices to coding and optimizing websites for visibility online, I’ve gained a well-rounded skill set to bring ideas to life.

Now I am looking forward to my next project. Maybe that will be with you?
